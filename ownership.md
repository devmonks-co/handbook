# Ownership

You are responsible for the work, not alone with it.

Asking is free and always will be. What isn't optional: you hold the work, its status, its risks and its unfinished edges, and anyone can learn the truth about it by asking you one question.

## What an owner does

1. **Knows the state of the work.** What's merged, in review, blocked and on whom, and whether the date holds. If someone has to ask twice, or piece it together from your PRs, you weren't holding it.
2. **Clarifies before building.** An unclear brief is yours to fix once you start typing. Ask, or write down the assumption you're working on and say it out loud. Silently guessing is the only wrong option.
3. **Brings bad news early, at full size.** Follow the [escalation rules](definition-of-done.md#when-to-raise-your-hand).
4. **Closes their own loops.** Review feedback is yours to turn around. If you're waiting on someone, chase them; waiting quietly isn't blocked, it's stalled. After merge, check that it works.
5. **Tells the truth about their own work first.** Name the weak part in the PR. Say when you're unsure or out of your depth. That's information, not weakness.

Nothing is punished less here than a self-reported problem. Nothing costs more than a hidden one.

## What ownership is not

| Not this | This |
|----------|------|
| Never asking for help | Asking early, then owning the outcome |
| Solving everything alone | Pulling the right person in, on time |
| Working late to hide a slip | Saying on Tuesday that Friday is at risk |
| Saying yes to every date | Saying what's realistic, then hitting it |

## The two-question test

At the end of any week, an owner answers both instantly:

1. **"What's the state of your work?"** A specific answer. No "mostly done".
2. **"What's the biggest risk right now, and who else knows?"** The honest answer is never "nothing".

## What you can expect from us

- **Fast answers when you escalate.** Your 2-hour rule needs a real response on our side.
- **Your PR stays yours.** We hand feedback back instead of pushing fixes onto your branch.
- **No silent takeovers.** If a task needs to move, we talk about scope with you, in writing.
- **Asking never costs you.** Not in tone, not in reviews, not in a joke.
